# Boundary Waters canoe atlas

Approved in conversation October 8, 2026. Six 17 x 11 inch landscape pages: overview, four overlapping route-detail sheets, and lake fisheries/source reference. Source geometry is the supplied 2008 Boundary Waters Trip KML. Preserve original route names and observations; do not reinterpret them as current records.

Map lake-depth contours where DNR data exist, identify survey vintage and gaps, and distinguish surveyed fish species, dated stocking records, historical personal observations, and inferred habitat. No fabricated fishing hot spots or missing-record interpretations.

Reserve margin space for square canoe-eye terrain views at incoming portage landings, with linked IDs, mapped viewpoints, viewing bearing, distance, and real vertical proportions. Portage matching and viewpoint locations are explicit derived products. Bare-earth views are experimental and do not represent treelines. Test lidar availability along route; show missing coverage rather than fill silently.

Deliver an offline QGIS project, PDF, source snapshots and links, build scripts, validation and visual review. Retain existing repo style and directory conventions. No push or public publication until build and review are complete.
