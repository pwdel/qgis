# Eastern Boundary Waters landmark evidence

Eight landmarks distinguish sights visible from water from waterfalls visited on foot. Coordinates identify either published points or explicitly broad regions; they are not validated compass-bearing targets. Accuracy values are conservative research estimates, not survey specifications.

| Landmark | Lake | Viewing mode | Coordinate quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clearwater Palisades | Clearwater | water-visible | region-anchor; ±850 m |
| Winchell cliffs | Winchell | water-visible | approximate-target; ±250 m |

## Clearwater Palisades

Western Clearwater Lake and the lodge waterfront, looking east; paddle toward the first cliff.

Evidence: The lodge describes two tall basalt cliffs dominating the eastward view from its waterfront and a paddle to the first cliff base. [Observation source](https://clearwaterhistoriclodge.com/gunflint-trail-activities/canoe-day-trips-2/).

Location: 48.075610, -90.350022. Published GPS is the landing WEST of the cliff. This is an anchor for a broad landmark region, not the cliff summit or face. [Coordinate source](https://bwcawild.com/BWCA-Hiking-Trails/Clearwater-Lake-Palisades-Trail/Clearwater-Lake-Palisades-Trail.html).

Do not use this coordinate as a precise target for a bearing or viewshed. The source describes two cliffs; record intentionally groups them.

## Winchell cliffs

Winchell Lake water north of the southern-shore cliffs; the field account explicitly describes looking at the cliffs from the water.

Evidence: First-person discussion and photographs document viewing the cliffs from water and a canoe landing to their west. [Observation source](https://bwca.com/index.cfm/index.cfm?confID=1&forumID=16&fuseaction=forum.thread&threadId=1019094).

Location: 47.989600, -90.619700. Published approximate point of interest named Cliffs of Winchell lake. [Coordinate source](https://bwca.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=maps.point_info&pt_id=3445&scode=N).

Community waypoint, not surveyed. Trail directions conflict across older accounts; no hiking route is asserted here.

## Rose Lake cliffs

Rose Lake western arm and the approach from Rat Lake; cliffs also appear eastward from western South Lake.

Evidence: Field guide describes tall shoreline cliffs and the scenic paddle through the long western arm. A canoe-trip photograph independently documents Rose cliffs eastward from South Lake. [Observation source](https://bwcawild.com/BWCA-Lakes/Gunflint-District/Rose-Lake/Rose-Lake.html).

Location: 48.101356, -90.472927. Published Rose Lake Cliffs overlook coordinate, used to anchor a broad cliff region. [Coordinate source](https://www.backpacker.com/trips/50-views-50-states-best-views-united-states?scope=anon).

Overlook coordinate is not a surveyed cliff face. Visibility is regional, not an all-lake or all-direction claim.

## Brule rockslide

Western Brule Lake bay beside the Wench portage; photographed from the lake at the published Brule Rockslide photo point.

Evidence: The paddler describes crossing to the rockslide, the striking escarpment, and landing beneath its cliffs at the Wench portage. [Observation source](https://bwca.com/index.cfm?fid=55&fuseaction=bwca_routes.trip_report&trid=3885).

Location: 47.947130, -90.746070. Published View from Brule Rockslide photo coordinate; the same album locates the on-water Brule Rockslide photo nearby. [Coordinate source](https://www.paddleplanner.com/photos/albumphotos.aspx?album=163).

Photo geotags are not surveyed feature geometry; approximate region only.

## Seagull Palisades

North side of Seagull Lake beside Miles Island; canoe past the cliff face.

Evidence: Outfitter explicitly describes canoeing past the palisades before stopping for a hike. [Observation source](https://voyageuroutfitters.com/resort-activities/).

Location: 48.141633, -90.921617. Outfitter publishes Seagull Palisades waypoint N 48 degrees 08.498 minutes, W 090 degrees 55.297 minutes; converted to decimal degrees. [Coordinate source](https://www.seagulloutfitters.com/gps-navigation/).

Waypoint may identify top rather than exposed face; lake visibility supported but no exact bearing validated.

## Rose Falls / Stairway Falls

Walk from the Duncan–Rose Stairway Portage landing; viewpoint is on land beside the falls.

Evidence: The lodge instructs visitors to leave the canoe at the landing and walk to the falls. [Observation source](https://clearwaterhistoriclodge.com/gunflint-trail-activities/canoe-day-trips-2/).

Location: 48.092230, -90.444720. OpenStreetMap waterfall node 5883313942 coordinates as published by Mapcarta. [Coordinate source](https://mapcarta.com/N5883313942).

Waterfall access is documented; no claim of seeing the falls from a canoe. Falls and trail viewpoints are different locations.

## Johnson Falls

Trail at far western Pine Lake, reached after the Little Caribou–Pine carry; about a quarter-mile walk to the falls.

Evidence: Outfitter describes a quarter-mile hike from the west end of Pine to the two-tier falls. [Observation source](https://clearwaterhistoriclodge.com/gunflint-trail-activities/canoe-day-trips-2/).

Location: 48.060400, -90.265300. Published approximate Lower Johnson Falls waypoint; this record groups both tiers. [Coordinate source](https://bwca.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=maps.point_info&pt_id=10035&scode=N).

Community point is approximate and marks lower falls. The source website miscategorizes it as Pictographs; that category is not adopted.

## Saganaga Falls

Beside the final Granite River–Saganaga carry; use the portage-side area as the viewing region.

Evidence: Outfitter describes arrival at Saganaga Falls and the 34-rod portage around them before entering Saganaga. [Observation source](https://voyageuroutfitters.com/granite/).

Location: 48.228510, -90.839710. Canadian Geographical Names Database official feature FCNHV, published 48.22851,-90.83971, with 100 m location descriptor; corroborated by GNIS/OSM-derived waterfall point 48.2286,-90.8398. [Coordinate source](https://geogratis.gc.ca/services/geoname/en/geonames.html?category=O%2CP%2CM&expand=items.status%2Citems.concise%2Citems.generic%2Citems.province&q=falls&select=items.status.term%2Citems.concise.term%2Citems.generic.term%2Citems.province.description&skip=1000&sort-field=name).

Conservatively classified as a portage stop; no safe open-water viewing position asserted. A separate GeoNames-derived Mapcarta point lies hundreds of metres northeast and was rejected.

## Rendering constraints

Use a regional landmark marker for Clearwater and Rose cliffs. Do not convert the Clearwater trail landing into a falsely precise cliff summit. Winchell, Brule, and Seagull have better published target anchors, but still require geometry and line-of-sight checks before drawing bearings. No record yet has a validated exact water-vantage/target pair.

Rose Falls, Johnson Falls, and Saganaga Falls need the walking/portage-stop symbol. They must not receive the water-visible cliff symbol. The source for Saganaga identifies the portage, not a surveyed pedestrian viewpoint.

## Deferred candidates

- **Magnetic Rock:** Found hiking access, but no evidence of visibility from a named lake.
- **Gunflint High Cliffs:** Sources establish an overlook above the lake, but not a specific from-water target and vantage pair.
- **Bridal Veil Falls:** Boat access followed by a short hike is supported; no corroborated target coordinate acquired in this pass. Not water-visible evidence. [Source](https://www.rockwoodbwca.com/bridal-veil-falls-hike/).
- **Kiskadinna shoreline cave:** Sawbill outfitter supports seeing a small cave on the south shoreline while paddling, but precise location not established; keep as lake-level research candidate. [Source](https://sawbill.com/routes/long-island-winchell-loop/).
