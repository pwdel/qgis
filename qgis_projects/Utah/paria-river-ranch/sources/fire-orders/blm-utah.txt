} Search Utah Prescribed Fire Utah - Fire Restrictions Twitter YouTube Facebook Instagram Flickr LinkedIn Home Programs Fire State Information Utah Utah - Fire Restrictions BLM Utah Fire Restrictions The Bureau of Land Management implements fire restrictions to prevent losses from human-caused wildfires brought on by extreme drought, high fire danger conditions, and the increased use of public lands. The BLM asks the public to use their Fire Sense to help prevent human-caused wildfires. Utah BLM districts are separated as follows. For specific state and district fire restriction information, see the drop-down options below this map. BLM Utah management districts Stage 2 Fire Restrictions These fire restrictions are in addition to stage 1 fire restrictions and include multiple measures to help keep public lands safe and accessible as the risk of human-caused wildfires increases. The BLM asks the public to use Fire Sense, a new statewide campaign, focused on common-sense practices to help prevent human-caused wildfires. Among the most important restrictions to follow includes campfires. During Stage 2 fire restrictions, the BLM does not allow campfires, even in permanently constructed cement or metal fire pits provided in agency-developed campground and picnic areas. Devices such as camp stoves and grills fueled by petroleum or liquid petroleum gas with a shut-off valve are allowed in all locations. Phase 2 restrictions include: No igniting, building, maintaining, or using a fire or open flame and no campfires using wood, charcoal and briquettes in designated and non-designated campgrounds.
