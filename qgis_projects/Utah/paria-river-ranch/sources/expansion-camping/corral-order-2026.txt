SONAL CAMPING RESTRICTIONS AT LIVESTOCK CORRALS 
PR
OHIBITIONS: 
Pursuant to Title 36 CFR 261.50(b), the following acts are prohibited at corrals described in this order on the Cedar 
City, Escalante, Pine Valley, Cedar City, and Powell Ranger Districts, Dixie National Forest, until further notice: 
1. Ca
mping within 300 ft (100 yds) of a livestock corral from May 1 to November 15 that is posted as
prohibited [Title 36 CFR 261.58(e)].
2. Pa
rking or leaving a vehicle in violation of posted instructions [Title 36 CFR 261.58(g)].
EXE
MPTIONS: 
Pursuant to Title 36 CFR 261.50(e), the following persons are exempted from the prohibition contained in this order:  
1. Persons with a permit specifically exempting them from the effect of this Order.
2. Any Federal, State, or local officer, or member of an organized rescue or fire fighting force in the
performance of an official duty.
3. Federal or State administrative personnel in the performance of an official duty.
AR
EA DESCRIBED: 
Corrals on the Cedar City, Escalante, Pine Valley, and Powell Ranger Districts, Dixie National Forest that are listed 
and described in Attachment 1 and depicted in Attachments 2-5 (maps) when posted with signs prohibiting camping 
or parking within 300 ft (100 yds). 
PURP
OSE/DURATION: 
This order is intended to reduce the disruption or harassment of livestock during gathering, loading or unloading 
activities associated with permitted livestock grazing. 
IMPLE
MENTATION: 
1. This Order will be in effect when signed and shall remain in effect until December  31, 2028, or until
rescinded, whichever occurs first.
2. Violation of the above prohibition(s) is punishable by a fine of not more than $5,000.00 for an
individual or $10,000.00 for an organization, and/or imprisonment for not more than six (6) m onths. [Title
16 USC 551, Title 18 USC 3571(b) (6), Title 18 USC 3581 (b) (7)].
3. Further information regarding this order may be obtained at the Pine Valley, Cedar City , Powell or
Escalante Ranger District offices located in St. George, Cedar City, Panguitch, and Escalante, Utah.  Pine
Valley (435) 652-3100, Cedar City (435) 865-3200, Powell, (435-676-9300), Escalante (435) 826-5499;
and at the Dixie National Forest Supervisor’s Office in Cedar City, Utah, (435) 865-3700.
Done at Cedar City, Utah this _16th__ day of _June_, 2026. 
KEVIN WRIGHT 
Forest Supervisor
