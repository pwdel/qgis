The order limits camping and motor vehicle travel to designated sites and roads.

Dave's Hollow - Camping Restrictions
PROHIBITIONS
Pursuant to 36 CFR § 261.50(a), the following acts are prohibited on the National Forest System (NFS) lands (“Restricted Area”) within the Powell Ranger District of the Dixie National Forest described below and shown on the attached map incorporated into this Order:
Camping in an undesignated area or site. [36 CFR § 261.58(e)]
Camping within 10 miles of the same location for more than 16 consecutive days, or for more than 16 days in a 60-day time period. [36 CFR § 261.58(a)]
Leaving camping equipment unattended for more than 24 hours in a designated area or site without permission of an authorized Forest Officer. [36 CFR § 261.16(i)]
EXEMPTIONS
Pursuant to 36 CFR § 261.50(e) the following persons are exempt from this Order:
Persons with a special use authorization or other Forest Service authorization specifically exempting them from the effect of this Order.
Any Federal, State, or local officer, or member of an organized rescue or fire fighting force in the performance of an official duty.
Federal or State administrative personnel in the performance of an official duty.
RESTRICTED AREA
The area affected by this order is described as portions of the Dave’s Hollow: N ½ NW ¼ Sec 20; North ¾ of Sec 19, T. 36 S, R 3 W; S ¾ Sec. 13, Sec. 24, NW ¼ Sec. 25, area east of road 088 in Sec. 26, area south and east of road 088 in section 34, area east of road 088 and south of road 096 Sec. 35, T 36 S, R 4 W. Section 2 within the forest boundary and section 3 east of East Creek, T 37 S, R4W. Salt Lake Base Meridian (See attached map.)
PURPOSE
This purpose of this Order is to facilitate resource protection and proper management of National Forest Lands and the adjacent private and National Park Service lands from recreation impacts associated with camping in the Dave’s Hollow area and to provide recreational opportunities to more members of the general public. The order limits camping and motor vehicle travel to designated sites and roads.
IMPLEMENTATION
This Order will be in effect on May 1, 2022 and shall remain in effect until May 1, 2027 or until rescinded, whichever occurs first.
A violation of the above prohibition(s) in this Order is punishable by a fine of not more than $5,000.00 for an individual or $10,000.00 for an organization, and/or imprisonment for not more than six (6) months. [Title 16 USC 551, Title 18 USC 3571(b) (6), Title 18 USC 3581 (b) (7)].
Further information regarding this Order may be obtained at the Dixie National Forest Powell Ranger District Office located in Panguitch, Utah telephone number 435-676-9300; and at the Dixie National Forest Supervisors Office in Cedar City, Utah telephone number 435-865-3700.
Done at Cedar City, UT this 11 day of April, 2022.
KEVIN WRIGHT
Forest Supervisor
