# Camping and ownership research

Snapshot: 2026-10-08. Evidence C01–C14 is in `sources/expansion-camping/camping-evidence.json`, with original URLs and effective dates. This research supports a separate ownership/camping-context sheet. It does not support an atlas-wide green “camping allowed” fill.

## Map-ready evidence

Official Utah surface ownership polygons were queried directly from the [Trust Lands feature service](https://gis.trustlands.utah.gov/mapping/rest/services/Land_Ownership/FeatureServer/0), linked by [UGRC](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/cadastre/land-ownership/). Files `ranch-ownership.geojson`, `barracks-ownership.geojson` and `red-canyon-ownership.geojson` retain original geometry and all attributes. Queries use the exact EPSG:26912 projected map envelopes in `02-ranch-extent.json`, `03-mount-carmel-extent.json`, and `04-red-canyon-extent.json` with `inSR=26912` and envelope-intersects; complete source features can extend outside the requested map. Clip in map display. GeoJSON is WGS84; source service metadata and exact query URLs are retained. Counts: 23, 24 and 25 features. Each response was verified against independent `returnCountOnly` and `returnIdsOnly` queries; exact object-ID sets match, without transfer-limit flags. Ownership is a best-effort 1:24,000 compilation, not a surveyed access right.

Ranch includes monument, wilderness, private and trust-land display categories; agency attributes identify BLM administration where applicable. Barracks includes BLM, private, trust and other state land. Red Canyon includes National Forest, BLM, private, trust and other state categories. `state_lgd` supplies display categories; `agency` and `admin` distinguish ownership from administration. Utah polygons do not establish ownership south of the state line.

`camping-points.geojson` contains the official BLM Barracks Kiosk reference point. It is a staging reference, not a designated campsite or horse-trailer fit determination. Existing atlas Coyote Hollow and ranch points may be reused with their existing coordinate provenance; no additional approximate camps were invented.

## Area advice

**Ranch / Paria:** distinguish the private ranch’s booked horse camping from public-land camping. GSENM requires a free overnight permit. Paria Canyon wilderness has a separate advance overnight permit and excludes motor vehicles. White House is advertised as car and walk-in camping, not an equestrian campground. No current authoritative backcountry horse-camp inventory was located. Do not convert a wilderness permit or a ranch ride list into trailer camping permission. See [GSENM](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/utah/grand-staircase-escalante-national-monument/plan) and [Paria Canyon permits](https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/permits-and-passes/lotteries-and-permit-systems/arizona/paria-canyon).

**Mt Carmel / Barracks / Bay Bill:** ownership context is particularly useful here because private and trust-land parcels interrupt BLM. [BLM’s Barracks Kiosk](https://www.blm.gov/visit/barracks-kiosk) documents dispersed camps along the river, no water at staging, private Barracks Ranch, and difficult vehicle travel beyond the ranch. It does not verify a particular trailer campsite, corral entitlement or turnout. Bay Bill remains a riding lead without verified campsite geometry. [Trust Lands](https://trustlands.utah.gov/trust-lands-and-you/) has its own camping limitations and parcel exceptions; BLM’s reference to camping on state land is not blanket permission.

**Red Canyon / Losee / Casto / Coyote Hollow / Tom Best:** [Coyote Hollow](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10381898) is the documented equestrian campground; use current reservation information, not the older brochure’s no-reservations statement. Its seasonal stock water may be depleted. General forest camping remains conditional on access, local orders and signs. The forest occupancy order 0407-25-01 runs to March 11, 2030: developed camping has a 14-day/30-day limit; other camping within ten miles of one location has a 16-day/60-day limit.

## Current restrictions and important expired documents

- **0407-26-04**, May 15, 2026–May 15, 2031: within 300 feet of designated water sources posted with camping/vehicle restriction signs on Powell and Cedar City districts. Official source HTML, PDF and Powell map JPEG retained. Do not buffer every hydrography feature: designation and posting matter. [Official alert](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/cedar-city-and-powell-ranger-districts-water-sources-camping-restrictions).
- **0407-26-08**, June 16, 2026–December 31, 2028: listed livestock corrals when posted; camping within 300 feet prohibited May 1–November 15. Attachment lists seven Powell corrals. These are livestock-gathering facilities, not public horse-camping facilities. Official PDF and extracted text retained. No precise coordinates or restriction buffers invented. [Official alert](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/seasonal-camping-restrictions-livestock-corrals).
- **0407-22-03**, through May 1, 2027: Dave’s Hollow mapped restriction limits camping to designated sites and unattended camping equipment to 24 hours without permission. Official map JPEG retained, without tracing a speculative polygon. [Official alert](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/powell-daves-hollow-camping-restrictions-order-0407-22-03).
- **0407-21-19 Tom Best expired May 15, 2026.** No replacement located through targeted official searches or the live Dixie alerts index. Expiration does not prove unrestricted camping. Old 150-foot roadside prose was not turned into a present-day polygon.
- **0407-21-12 East Fork Sevier expired June 30, 2026**, despite remaining on the live alerts index. Its designated-site rule must not be presented as a verified current order. This river is near Tropic Reservoir, unrelated to the East Fork Virgin at Barracks. [Still-listed expired alert](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/powell-east-fork-sevier-river-camping-restrictions-order-0407-21-12).

Direct retrieval of legacy Powell `fseprd1016115.pdf`, East Fork `fseprd746989.pdf`, and Tom Best order returned 404. Search-index text survives, but is not current legal proof. The modern USFS alert pages were retrieved through HTTPS Python requests after browser retrieval returned 403. Live alerts and source HTML are retained for audit. Restriction JPEGs are authoritative reference images, not georeferenced GIS boundaries.

## Suggested map treatment

Use muted ownership polygons, prominent private-land boundaries, and separate symbols for designated equestrian camping and staging. Put the current posted-water and corral restrictions in a compact note. Label Tom Best “current local camping restriction unresolved; old order expired 2026-05-15.” Keep expired restriction polygons off the current map unless clearly isolated as historical context. Current source geometry is insufficient to draw exact camp-permission boundaries. A map showing these limits is useful; a supposed legal campsite inventory would overstate the evidence.
