# Expanded atlas sources and interpretation

Research snapshot: October 8, 2026. Source footers on all expanded sheets link here. The original E01–E19 citations remain in [the original source register](SOURCES.md). New evidence is machine-readable in [expansion-evidence.json](../sources/expansion-evidence.json); camping records preserve order terms and retrieval status separately.

## Terrain and snow

- **T01 — [Trail-adjacent terrain screening](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer)**, USGS 3DEP / UGRC; atlas derivation. Percent cell slope within150m of mapped trails and terrain below selected trail raster cells within100m. 10-15m output service cells; native contributing resolution unresolved. Not trail-tread grade, surveyed cliff height or horse-safety classification.
- **S01 — [NOAA station snow normals](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals)**, NOAA NCEI. 1991-2020 monthly snowfall normals and midmonth probability of observed depth>=1inch; station elevations and completeness flags. Point data, varying valid year counts; not spatial interpolation or trip probability.
- **S02 — [Monthly satellite snow-cover ensemble](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20610475)**, Celik & Hengl / OpenGeoHub. Published monthly p50 snow-cover ensemble of DLR and ESA inputs, clipped without resampling. Exploratory, initial accuracy; average of source medians; component periods differ; ESA processing partly unreproduced; not calibrated snow-day chance or snow depth.
- **S03 — [ENSO context for Utah](https://statesummaries.ncics.org/chapter/ut/)**, NOAA / NCICS. El Nino and La Nina can affect broad winter patterns; Utah response is inconsistent. No route-specific October forecast or numeric ENSO adjustment is supported.

S02 attribution: Mehmet Furkan Çelik and Tomislav Hengl, OpenGeoHub foundation (2026), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20610475, CC BY 4.0. Derived from [DLR Global SnowPack](https://download.geoservice.dlr.de/GSP/files/daily/) and [ESA CCI MODIS snow](https://data.ceda.ac.uk/neodc/esacci/snow/data/scfg/MODIS/v4.0). Regional subsets retain original grid cells. Archived processing notebooks retain the upstream MIT [code license](../sources/expansion-snow/CODE-LICENSE.txt). The atlas changes styling and adds independent trail/road layers. See [full snow research and processing caveats](snow-research.md) and [raster metadata](../sources/expansion-snow/gridded-snow-metadata.json).

S01 station CSV URLs, contributing years, measurement/completeness flags, and definitions are preserved in [snow-climatology.json](../sources/expansion-snow/snow-climatology.json). October station percentages are the NOAA daily normal for the 15th, calculated in a centered 29-day window. They are not the chance of encountering snow during a 29-day visit. S03 also uses [National Weather Service ENSO guidance](https://www.weather.gov/twc/enso).

## Camping / ownership

- **C01 — [Utah surface ownership](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/cadastre/land-ownership/)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Best-effort 1:24,000 surface ownership, maintained by SITLA/BLM and partners; ownership differs from management or jurisdiction. Polygons are context, never permission to camp or cross private land.
- **C02 — [Barracks Kiosk](https://www.blm.gov/visit/barracks-kiosk)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Several dispersed camping sites are described along the river. Barracks Ranch is private. Kiosk is unimproved staging with no water or other amenities. Roads beyond ranch are not recommended for street or two-wheel-drive vehicles. Published kiosk coordinate is a reference point, not a campsite polygon or measured horse-trailer turnaround.
- **C03 — [Coyote Hollow](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10381898)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Equestrian campground; current listing makes all sites reservable with equestrian priority. Seasonal nonpotable stock water can run out in late summer or early fall; Red Canyon Campground dump station water is the published fallback. Older USFS equestrian brochure saying no reservations is outdated relative to current listing.
- **C04 — [Dixie occupancy and use](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r04/dixie/publication/alerts/0407-25-01-Forestwide-OccupancyAndUse-Order.pdf)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Developed sites: at most 14 consecutive days and 14 days per 30-day period. Camping within 10 miles of same location: at most 16 consecutive days and 16 days per 60-day period. Closed developed sites and parking against posted instructions prohibited.
- **C05 — [Powell and Cedar City posted water sources](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/cedar-city-and-powell-ranger-districts-water-sources-camping-restrictions)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Camping and parking within 300 feet of designated water sources posted with the respective restriction signs are prohibited. Not a blanket buffer around every mapped stream, spring, trough or campground hydrant.
- **C06 — [Seasonal livestock corral restrictions](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/seasonal-camping-restrictions-livestock-corrals)**. Retrieval: retrieved. At listed corrals when posted, no camping within 300 feet May 1–November 15; obey posted parking instructions. Powell list: Daves Hollow #306502, Daves Hollow G.S. #306501, East Fork #306504, Day #313500, Mud Springs #317502 and #317501, Permittee #306505. The BLM Barracks corrals are not inferred to be covered by this USFS order.
- **C07 — [Tom Best historic roadside restriction](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r04/dixie/publication/alerts/0407-21-19-Powell-TomBestAreaCampingRestrct-ORIGINAL.pdf)**. Retrieval: search-index text; direct URL 404. Historical restriction on signed mapped portions of roads 117,130,646 and 150 feet either side. Expired May 15, 2026. No replacement was located on live alerts index or targeted official searches. This does not prove camping is currently permitted.
- **C08 — [East Fork Sevier historic restriction](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/powell-east-fork-sevier-river-camping-restrictions-order-0407-21-12)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Order limited camping to designated sites in its mapped corridor and unattended equipment to 24 hours. Its own term ended June 30, 2026 even though the alert remains listed. No replacement located. This East Fork is the Sevier River near Tropic Reservoir, not the East Fork Virgin River at Barracks.
- **C09 — [Daves Hollow camping](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/alerts/powell-daves-hollow-camping-restrictions-order-0407-22-03)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Mapped restriction area allows camping only at designated areas/sites; unattended camping equipment limited to 24 hours without officer permission. Official map retained as reference; no polygon was reconstructed from township prose.
- **C10 — [GSENM trip planning](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/utah/grand-staircase-escalante-national-monument/plan)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Free permits are required for all overnight stays in GSENM, available at visitor centers or developed trailheads. This does not designate horse-trailer parking or waive site-specific restrictions.
- **C11 — [Paria Canyon permits](https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/permits-and-passes/lotteries-and-permit-systems/arizona/paria-canyon)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Backcountry overnight permits must be reserved in advance; use existing campsites at least 200 feet from water; Wrather Canyon closed to camping. Wilderness excludes motor vehicles: a backcountry permit never authorizes a trailer camp inside it. Campfires prohibited; pack out human waste. Current page does not establish horse-specific camp locations.
- **C12 — [Utah Trust Lands recreation](https://trustlands.utah.gov/trust-lands-and-you/)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Camping allowed on most trust land for up to 15 consecutive days; some areas have 7-day limits or closures. Use existing durable campsites at least 100 feet from water. Do not leave equipment unattended more than 72 hours; motorized parking over 100 feet from authorized routes prohibited. Trust lands are not public lands; verify parcel-specific access. Current page says open campfires prohibited.
- **C13 — [White House campground](https://www.blm.gov/visit/white-house-campground)**. Retrieval: retrieved. Published as seven car-camping and five walk-in tent sites. Not documented as an equestrian campground; do not assume horse or long-trailer accommodation.
- **C14 — [Powell camping overview](https://www.fs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/fseprd1016115.pdf)**. Retrieval: search index only; direct URL 404. Search-index map labels Tom Best, East Fork and Daves Hollow restriction areas. Old overview is not proof of currently effective restrictions; Tom Best and East Fork cited orders expired in 2026.

[Camping research](camping-research.md) explains current versus expired orders, ownership scale, and why unsupported restriction buffers were not drawn. Source polygons are ownership context, not permission to camp.
