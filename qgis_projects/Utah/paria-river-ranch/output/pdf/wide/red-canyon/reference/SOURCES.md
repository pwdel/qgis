# Sources and claim register

Research snapshot: 2026-10-07. IDs printed on the PDF resolve here. Click each source title for its original source. A publication/download is not a current field inspection.

## E01 — [Horse camping](https://pariariverranch.com/horse-camping-1)

Publisher: Paria River Ranch. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Ranch advertises horse camping, potable water for people and horses, and rides/haul-outs.

Limits: Owner advertising; no live flow test or exact-rig turning assessment. Route names do not confer public-land permission.

## E02 — [Paria River Ranch location](https://www.aaa.com/tripcanvas/campground/paria-river-ranch-31872)

Publisher: AAA / Roverpass. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Ranch reference coordinate 37.106604, -111.912501 and address.

Limits: Third-party facility reference; not a surveyed gate, hydrant or parking stall.

## E03 — [Coyote Hollow](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10381898)

Publisher: Recreation.gov. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Four seasonal nonpotable horse-water hydrants may be available; supply can be exhausted late summer/fall. Equestrian camping; fallback water described at Red Canyon Campground.

Limits: Campground supply differs from trail water. No present flow or refill capacity verification.

## E04 — [White House Campground](https://www.blm.gov/visit/white-house-campground)

Publisher: Bureau of Land Management. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Drinking water is described at Paria Contact Station, not at White House campsites.

Limits: Does not establish horse-trailer turnaround, bulk-water fill permission, or current operating hours.

## E05 — [Backcountry Rides in the Kanab Area](https://www.bchutah.org/High%20Desert/2022-2-4-Backcountry-Rides-Kanab-Area.pdf)

Publisher: Back Country Horsemen of Utah. Source: PDF. Status: Retrieved.

Page 1 describes Barracks corrals staging and warns that farther down the road trailer parking/turning is poor; Bay Bill is a riding lead. Page 2 warns about hauling up the Old Paria access grade.

Limits: Historical community riding report, not current owner permission or measured rig fit. Do not reuse its broad claims about riding anywhere on BLM land.

## E06 — [Thunder Mountain trail sheet 33098](https://www.fs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/stelprdb5436859.pdf)

Publisher: USDA Forest Service. Source: PDF. Status: SearchIndexOnly_Original404.

Indexed USFS description reports narrow exposed terrain, drop-offs and no water on the trail.

Limits: Original URL returned HTTP 404 on retrieval; claim retained as a search-index lead needing current manager confirmation. Not field-verified.

## E07 — [Belly of the Dragon](https://www.visitsouthernutah.com/11-breathtaking-things-to-do-in-kanab-utah/)

Publisher: Kane County tourism. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Hiking tunnel reference coordinate and drainage/flash-flood warning.

Limits: Hiking attraction, not verified horse passage. Newer tourism location record differs; coordinate is approximate and disputed.

## E08 — [Utah trailheads](https://services1.arcgis.com/99lidPhWCzftIe9K/ArcGIS/rest/services/UtahTrailheads/FeatureServer/0)

Publisher: UGRC. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Published trailhead reference coordinates, retaining source IDs.

Limits: Dataset records here derive from OrbitalView; LastUpdate and access facilities may be null. No evidence of trailer fit.

## E09 — [Utah roads](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/transportation/road-centerlines/)

Publisher: UGRC / local stewards / UDOT. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Road geometry and CARTOCODE including high-clearance code 16.

Limits: Road line/class does not establish public passage, current surface, road grade or trailer compatibility.

## E10 — [Utah trails and pathways](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/recreation/trails-pathways/)

Publisher: UGRC. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Trail geometry and explicit HorseAllowed attributes.

Limits: Incomplete network, mixed-source mapping; original observation dates generally unresolved. Does not supersede closures.

## E11 — [National Forest System trails](https://apps.fs.usda.gov/arcx/rest/services/EDW/EDW_TrailNFSPublishWithDataStatus_01/MapServer/0)

Publisher: USDA Forest Service. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Trail centerlines, IDs, named segments, tread-width categories and use attributes.

Limits: Blank pack/saddle fields are unknown. Tread width is a published category, not a current measurement; typical_trail_grade contains a code, not a numeric grade.

## E12 — [Utah lidar coverage](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/elevation/lidar/)

Publisher: UGRC. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Completed 2018 Southern Utah and 2019 Kane County projects include sampled locations.

Limits: Coverage index is not a terrain raster or present-condition survey; exact per-pixel coverage must be checked.

## E13 — [USGS Topo](https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSTopo/MapServer)

Publisher: USGS. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Topographic reference context with roads, place names, hydrography and terrain.

Limits: Mixed-date compilation; blue streams are mapped hydrography, not verified drinking supplies.

## E14 — [3DEP elevation service](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer)

Publisher: USGS. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Bare-earth elevation mosaic used for regional hillshade and slope context.

Limits: Resampled service mosaic. Output pixel size does not establish native resolution, acquisition date or vertical accuracy.

## E15 — [Encouraging your horse to drink](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/horse/encouraging-your-horse-to-drink)

Publisher: University of Minnesota Extension. Source: Website. Status: Retrieved.

Typical 1000-pound horse drinks about 8-10 gallons daily; exercise and heat increase needs.

Limits: General husbandry information; five gallons is not a daily upper limit or a sufficient trip-specific allocation.

## E16 — [Tom Best camping order 0407-21-19](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r04/dixie/publication/alerts/0407-21-19-Powell-TomBestAreaCampingRestrct-ORIGINAL.pdf)

Historic order describes restricted roadside camping areas and ends May 15, 2026 by its terms.

## E17 — [Mount Carmel US Topo quadrangle](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/USTopo/Current/PDF/UT/UT_Mount_Carmel.pdf)

## Standards

- [OGC GeoPackage 1.4](https://www.geopackage.org/spec140/): portable QGIS vectors; metadata extension is a possible future integration, not a claimed water standard.
- [Project governance](GOVERNANCE.md): critical-claim requirements, unresolved states and review policy.

## Rights

USGS and USFS public federal GIS sources are attributed. UGRC publishes statewide GIS for reuse; attribution and source limitations are retained. Recreation.gov facility metadata is attributed. Third-party ranch, tourism and riding-guide statements are cited and briefly paraphrased; their full artwork and original documents are not republished. This public atlas contains newly prepared Utah planning references, not the private Elm locations.
